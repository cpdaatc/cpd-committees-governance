Governance Framework

How the Committees Operate

CPD governance connects educational priorities, scientific integrity, accreditation coordination, activity delivery, evaluation and provider reaccreditation evidence through clear decision rights and documented accountability.

Mandate Responsibilities KPI Monitoring Risk Controls Reporting Improvement Plan
Define ScopeConfirm authority, coverage, exclusions, and reporting line.
Standardize OperationsUse approved agendas, minutes, trackers, and decision logs.
Monitor CPD PerformanceTrack application review, activity closure, overdue reports, evaluation evidence and improvement actions.
Escalate & ImproveConvert findings into corrective actions, owners, deadlines, and evidence.

Decision rights

Approved terms of reference, delegated authority, quorum, voting, recusals, escalation and documented decisions. Committees recommend unless authority is explicitly delegated.

Three Lines

Operational owners manage risk and controls. Risk and compliance functions support and challenge management. Internal audit provides independent assurance and must retain independence.

Evidence governance

Maintain a controlled register of decisions, action owners, deadlines, evidence, access permissions, retention and version history.

Proposed CPD governance structure

CPD Committees & Workstreams

SCFHS distinguishes provider-level scientific review from activity-level scientific planning. Other workstreams below are proposed internal arrangements, not mandated committees.

CPD Steering Committee

Oversee CPD strategy, annual plan, priorities, delegated decisions, risks and performance; escalate matters outside authority.

Provider Scientific Committee / External Scientific Expertise

Provider-level scientific content review; documented external scientific collaboration is an accepted alternative. Provider accreditation standard 3; renewal 3.2.

Accreditation & Compliance

Coordinate applicable SCFHS submissions, verify required evidence, track decisions and provider reaccreditation obligations.

Activity Operations

Coordinate readiness, registration, attendance verification, delivery and documented changes.

Evaluation & Impact

Monitor receipt of organizer-submitted completion reports, survey analysis and applicable impact evidence; track improvement actions.

CPD Planning & Improvement

Consolidate training needs, propose annual calendar, monitor gaps and evaluate improvements.

Scientific Planning Committee (SPC)

Required for each activity: at least two qualified subject experts; accountable for needs, objectives, learning methods, faculty, content, delivery and outcomes. Activity standard 1.1.

End-to-end CPD workflow

CPD Activity Lifecycle

Needs assessment & planningIdentify practice gaps and target learners; prioritize activities in the annual CPD plan.
Activity applicationOrganizer submits SPC membership, documented learning needs, SMART objectives, intended outcomes, agenda, delivery methods, faculty CVs and disclosures.
Scientific & compliance reviewDocument review, exceptions, corrections and decision within delegated authority.
Accreditation coordinationSubmit applicable requests through authorized channels; record the decision and approved activity details before representing it as accredited.
Delivery & attendanceOrganizer delivers the approved activity and maintains verified attendance and change records. All persons controlling content disclose relevant financial relationships to the provider and learners before the activity; commercial sponsorship and conflicts are disclosed in promotional materials.
Evaluation & completion reportOrganizer collects session and overall evaluations, assesses learning change using suitable methods, analyzes results and submits completion evidence. CPD receives, verifies and follows up; it does not replace the organizer in report preparation.
Closure & improvementDesignated authority confirms closure after evidence verification; track corrective actions and archive records for provider reaccreditation review.
Committee Profile

Continuing Professional Development Committee

Request IntakeReceive CPD activity request with scientific content, objectives, target audience, speakers, and proposed date.
Academic ReviewAssess educational value, learning objectives, relevance to practice, and alignment with institutional priorities.
Compliance ValidationCheck disclosures, conflict of interest, speaker documentation, attendance method, and required forms.
Decision & Follow-upRecord a recommendation or decision within delegated authority; otherwise escalate. Track post-activity report and impact evidence.

Core Deliverables

  • Reviewed CPD activity applications with documented decisions.
  • Approved activity calendar aligned to institutional needs.
  • Disclosure and declaration control records.
  • Post-activity evaluation summary and improvement actions.
  • Evaluate change for each activity using methods aligned with learning objectives; Kirkpatrick is an optional internal framework, not a named SCFHS requirement.

CPD closure control

The activity provider submits the completion report, survey results, analysis and impact evidence as applicable. The CPD function verifies completeness, records exceptions and follows up; the designated authority confirms closure. Verify applicable SCFHS provider and activity requirements against controlled current documents before approval.

Accountability boundaries

Assign one accountable owner per decision or control through an approved RACI. Operational management owns implementation; the committee monitors or recommends within its mandate. Independent assurance remains outside operational ownership. Validate all proposed roles with leadership.

Mustamir service deadlines

Conference

Pre-book at least one month before the event; apply for accreditation 21 working days before delivery.

Activity changes

Content or speaker changes: 7 days before start. Date or participant count changes: 24 hours before start.

Attendance

Register hours within 5 days after activity end. A 3-day extension may be requested within 30 days after the original registration period ends.

Reaccreditation

Apply 3–6 months before expiry. Submit self-assessment for the previous accreditation period (three years) and evidence of implementation.
